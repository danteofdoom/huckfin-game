How to Play

You're Huck Finn, drifting down the Mississippi River from the north all the way to New Orleans (mile 1000). Catch fish, sell them for dollars, hire companions, and buy upgrades to travel faster.

Complete the full journey to prestige — earn Rep and spend it on permanent Freedom Upgrades that carry over into every future run.

Upgrades

Upgrades are permanent for the current run and unlock in chains — you must own the previous one first.

CategoryUpgrades
🎣 FishingBetter Line → Two Lines → Fishing Net → Trotline* (more fish/sec)
🪵 RaftSturdy → Painted → Steam-Powered → Paddlewheel Riverboat* (more miles/sec)
💵 TradeTown Dock → Haggle → Known Trader → River Merchant / Trading Post Network* (fish worth more)
🤫 RiskLay Low (suspicion grows 50% slower)

* Unlocks after reaching a certain mile marker.

Prestige

When you reach New Orleans (mile 1000) you complete the journey. Your run resets but you earn ⭐ Rep based on dollars earned:

Rep = floor(dollars ÷ 200) × Rep multiplier

Spend Rep on Freedom Upgrades — permanent bonuses that apply to every future run.

UpgradeCostEffect
🏃 Lucky Cast1 ⭐Manual fish catches give 3 fish instead of 1
🌊 Faster Current2 ⭐+10% miles/sec base
🌟 Good Reputation2 ⭐Town bonuses +25%
🎣 Seasoned Fisherman3 ⭐Start with Fishing Net already owned
🗺️ Lay of the Land3 ⭐Suspicion capped at 80% — raids happen less often
📖 River Wisdom4 ⭐+3 bonus Rep on every prestige
👥 Full Crew5 ⭐All companions cost 50% less

Tips

  • Hire Jim first — free fishing income from the very start.
  • Buy Sturdy Raft early so miles accumulate while you sleep.
  • Avoid hiring the King before Vicksburg (mile 400) unless you have Lay Low.
  • The game saves automatically every tick — safe to close the tab.
  • Offline earnings are capped at 8 hours.
  • First prestige goal: earn enough Rep for Lucky Cast and Faster Current.
