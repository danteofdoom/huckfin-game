🛖 Huck Finn's Raft

How to Play

The Basics

You're Huck Finn. You've got a raft, a fishing line, and a thousand miles of river ahead of you. The goal is simple: drift down the Mississippi from the north all the way to New Orleans.

To get there you need money. To make money you catch fish and sell them. To catch more fish you hire companions and buy upgrades. The further you travel, the more becomes available to you.

Fish & Dollars

Tap the 🎣 Fish button to catch fish by hand. Once you have fish, tap 💵 Sell Fish to convert them into dollars.

Dollars are spent on companions and upgrades — everything that makes the game run itself. Early on you'll be tapping a lot. That changes quickly once Jim is aboard.

💡 The Trade upgrades (Town Dock, Haggle, Known Trader) multiply how much each fish is worth when you sell. Prioritise these once you have steady fishing income.

Miles & the River

The progress bar at the top shows how far down the river you are. Miles accumulate passively — but only once you've bought Raft upgrades. Without them the raft drifts at 0 miles per second and you'll never reach New Orleans.

Buy the Sturdy Raft as soon as you can afford it. From there, each raft upgrade multiplies how fast you travel.

Companions

Companions join your raft and earn resources every second without you doing anything. They unlock as you travel further downriver.

  • 👨 Jim — free from the start. Fishes for you passively.
  • 🎩 Tom Sawyer — appears at mile 100. Sells fish and earns dollars directly.
  • 👑 The King and 🎭 The Duke — appear at mile 200 (Memphis). High earners, but they come with a catch.
  • 🕯️ Widow Douglas — appears at mile 600 (Natchez). Boosts your Rep earnings on prestige.

Suspicion

The King and the Duke are con men. The moment you hire either of them a Suspicion meter appears. It fills up over time as they run their schemes.

⚠️ When Suspicion reaches 100%, the law catches up with you. You lose 30% of your dollars and the meter resets. This will keep happening until you deal with it.

To manage suspicion: buy the Lay Low upgrade, which halves the rate it fills. The King raises suspicion faster than the Duke — hiring both at once is risky early on.

If you don't want to deal with suspicion at all, simply don't hire the King or the Duke. You'll earn less dollars per second but the run will be smoother.

Towns

As you pass through towns along the river, a one-time event triggers. Most are good — a bonus payout, unlocking a new companion, or a permanent price boost. One can go badly if the King is on your raft.

You can see the next town and its mile marker at the top of the river bar. Plan around them — arriving at Vicksburg without the King on board means an easy $500.

Prestige

When you reach New Orleans (mile 1000) the journey is complete. The run resets — your fish, dollars, miles, companions and upgrades all go back to zero.

In return you earn ⭐ Rep, based on how many dollars you made during the run. The more dollars, the more Rep. Having the Widow Douglas on board increases the Rep you earn.

Rep is spent in the Freedom Upgrades panel, which appears after your first prestige. These upgrades are permanent — they apply at the start of every future run and make each journey faster and more profitable.

💡 On your first prestige, aim for Head Start (Jim hired from the start) and Faster Current (+10% miles/sec). They make the second run noticeably quicker.

Saving & Offline Progress

The game saves automatically every second. It's safe to close the tab at any time and pick up where you left off.

When you return, the game calculates how long you were away and credits you with the passive income (fish, dollars, miles) you would have earned — up to a maximum of 8 hours.

How to Play

You're Huck Finn, drifting down the Mississippi River from the north all the way to New Orleans (mile 1000). Catch fish, sell them for dollars, hire companions, and buy upgrades to travel faster.

Complete the full journey to prestige — earn Rep and spend it on permanent Freedom Upgrades that carry over into every future run.

Prestige

When you reach New Orleans (mile 1000) you complete the journey. Your run resets but you earn ⭐ Rep based on dollars earned:

Rep = floor(dollars ÷ 200) × Rep multiplier

Tips

  • Hire Jim first — free fishing income from the very start.
  • Buy Sturdy Raft early so miles accumulate while you sleep.
  • Avoid hiring the King before Vicksburg (mile 400) unless you have Lay Low.
  • The game saves automatically every tick — safe to close the tab.
  • Offline earnings are capped at 8 hours.
  • First prestige goal: earn enough Rep for Head Start and Faster Current.
